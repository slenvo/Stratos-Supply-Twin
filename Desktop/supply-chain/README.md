# ⚡ SANKALP: Energy Supply Chain Digital Twin

**AI-Driven Energy Supply Chain Resilience for Import-Dependent Economies**[cite: 1]

Sankalp is a geospatial intelligence platform designed to continuously monitor geopolitical risks, model disruption scenarios, and orchestrate adaptive procurement rerouting[cite: 1]. Built for the OOSC 4.0 Hackathon.

## 🎯 The Problem
India imports approximately 88% of its crude oil, with major volumes transiting high-risk chokepoints like the Strait of Hormuz and the Red Sea[cite: 1]. Current supply chain tools fail to model geopolitical risks in real-time or coordinate rapid rerouting strategies[cite: 1].

## 🚀 Key Features (MVP)
*   **Geospatial Digital Twin:** A live, interactive map visualization of global energy networks and shipping corridors[cite: 1].
*   **Disruption Scenario Modeller:** Instantly simulates the cascading impacts of chokepoint closures (e.g., Hormuz or Red Sea suspensions)[cite: 1].
*   **Strategic Reserve Optimizer:** Tracks the 9.5-day national consumption buffer and models emergency drawdown schedules against forecasted supply gaps[cite: 1].

## 💻 Tech Stack
*   **Frontend:** Next.js (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS
*   **Geospatial Engine:** React-Leaflet, OpenStreetMap/CartoDB
*   **UI Components:** Lucide-React
