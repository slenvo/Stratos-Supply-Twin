# ⚡ STRATOS (Strategic Tracking & Operational Supply): Energy Supply Chain Digital Twin

**AI-Driven Energy Supply Chain Resilience for Import-Dependent Economies**

STRATOS is a geospatial intelligence platform designed to continuously monitor geopolitical risks, model disruption scenarios, and orchestrate adaptive procurement rerouting. Built specifically to tackle the Challenge Statement for the OOSC 4.0 Hackathon.

## 🎯 The Problem

India imports approximately 88% of its crude oil, with major volumes transiting high-risk chokepoints like the Strait of Hormuz and the Red Sea. Current supply chain tools fail to model geopolitical risks in real-time or coordinate rapid rerouting strategies, leaving strategic reserves vulnerable to sudden closures.

## 🚀 Key Features (MVP)

- **Geospatial Digital Twin:** A live, interactive map visualization of global energy networks and shipping corridors powered by CartoDB Dark Matter for command-center level tracking.
- **Geopolitical Risk Intelligence Agent:** Live calculation of supply-disruption probability scores based on simulated corridor events and market panic scenarios.
- **Disruption Scenario Modeller:** Instantly simulates the cascading impacts of chokepoint closures (e.g., Hormuz or Red Sea suspensions) on refining capacity, price, and GDP.
- **Adaptive Procurement Orchestrator:** Automatically ranks alternative crude sources (e.g., West African or South American crude) and logistics routes for rapid rerouting recommendations.
- **Strategic Reserve Optimization Agent:** Tracks the national consumption buffer and models optimal emergency drawdown schedules against forecasted supply gaps.

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS
- **Geospatial Engine:** React-Leaflet, OpenStreetMap / CartoDB
- **UI Components:** Lucide-React
- **Deployment:** Vercel
